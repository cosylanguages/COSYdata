# P83 B1 derivatives — review notes

These are the forms included in the first pass that are correct but uncommon,
or for which a second opinion would help. Nothing here is known to be wrong; the
task rule is that a doubtful form is left out, so the entries below were kept
only because a dictionary or a productive pattern supports them.

## English

- `en:unfulfilled:adjective` → `unfulfillment` (noun). Rare, but formed
  regularly from `unfulfilled`; a reviewer may prefer to drop it.
- `en:enable:verb` → `enablement` (noun). Attested in business/technical use.
- `en:compostable:adjective` → `compostability`; `en:renewable:adjective` →
  `renewability`; `en:approachable:adjective` → `approachability`;
  `en:challenging:adjective` → `challengingly`; `en:sophisticated:adjective` →
  `sophisticatedly`; `en:observant:adjective` → `observantly`. Low-frequency
  derivatives, kept for completeness of the family.
- `en:literal:adjective` → `literalism`; `en:definite:adjective` →
  `definiteness`; `en:diverse:adjective` → `diversely`. Correct but bookish.
- `en:vital:adjective` → `vitally`. Adverbial use is standard, though less
  common than `vitality`.

## Russian

- `ru:deli:noun` → `делийский` (adjective). Regular from `Дели`, but the
  conventional adjective is uncommon.
- `ru:pokolenie:noun` → `поколенческий`. Attested, but `поколенческий` is
  journalistic; some dictionaries prefer a phrase.
- `ru:rekruter:noun` → `рекрутинг`. Borrowed noun, common in HR usage but not in
  every dictionary.

## Greek

- `el:kariera:noun` → `καριερίστας`. Common colloquial noun; standard dictionaries
  differ on register.
- `el:sygkroysi:noun` → `συγκρουσιακός`. Regular adjective, mostly used in
  political/social contexts.
- `el:eisodima:noun` → `εισοδηματικός`. Regular adjective, mainly in economics.
- `el:exoythenosi:noun` → `εξουθενωμένος`. The participle is more common than a
  finite passive; kept as the adjectival family member.

## Italian

- `it:usanza:noun` → `usualmente` (adverb). This was first written
  `usualemente` and corrected; the accepted form is `usualmente`.
- `it:transazione:noun` → `transazionale`; `it:pensione:noun` →
  `pensionistico`; `it:laboratorio:noun` → `laboratoriale`. Regular, but
  specialist vocabulary.
- `it:saggio:noun` → `saggista`. The noun sense here is "essay", so the family
  member is the person, not a derived abstract noun.

## French

- `fr:bretagne:noun` → `breton` (adjective), `Breton` (noun). The capitalised
  noun is the inhabitant; the lowercase adjective follows French usage.
- `fr:bnvole:noun` → `bénévolat`, `bénévolement`. The headword id lost its
  accents upstream; the derivatives use the correct spelling.

## B2 review notes

### English

- English B2 links were proposed from WordNet derivational relations. The
  following were removed by hand because they are synonyms or unrelated words,
  not family members: `dismissal` → `fire`, `sack`; `contend` → `contest`;
  `monetary` → `money`; `revolution` → `revolt`, `revolve`; `underline` →
  `underscore`; `standpoint` → `stand`; `signify` → `sign`.
- Rare but attested forms kept: `compliancy`, `assertable`, `accommodator`,
  `arbitrament`, `vividness`, `enormousness`. A reviewer may prefer to drop the
  rarest of these.
- `en:enormous:adjective` → `enormity` carries `meaning_shift`
  ("a very evil act"), because the modern sense is not "large size".
- 386 English B2 entries still have no field. Most are nouns with no
  single-word family (`firewall`, `gpa`, `spectrum`) or verbs with no derived
  noun (`comprise`, `overlook`, `outweigh`). A later manual pass should decide
  whether to add a family member from outside the current lexicon.
- Adverbs were added only when WordNet contains the adverb. This avoids
  non-words such as `*doctoral+ly` patterns that a spelling rule would create.

### Polysemy pilot

- Three passes added 71 English sense entries. Pass 1: `court` sport and
  `light` not heavy A2; `bank` river, `match` game, `spring` water, `case`
  instance, `point` idea, `state` condition and `matter` be important B1;
  `season` add flavour, `charge` accusation, `issue` release and `right`
  entitlement B2. Pass 2: 12 A2 senses (`set`, `turn`, `line`, `drop`, `note`,
  `pass`, `plan`, `press`, `ring`, `top`, `treat`, `way`) and 19 B1 senses
  (`order`, `cover`, `face`, `head`, `live`, `look`, `mean`, `paper`, `part`,
  `raise`, `rest`, `room`, `store`, `strike`, `support`, `term`, `train`,
  `wear`, `wonder`). Pass 3: 10 A2 senses (`work` operate, `play` music,
  `call` shout, `play` theatre, `break` rest, `point` score, `form` shape,
  `light` pale, `keep` continue, `set` put), 15 B1 senses (`work` artwork,
  `call` name, `hold` contain, `hold` event, `keep` record, `break` rule,
  `check` inspection, `fire` shoot, `form` type, `run` manage, `run` series,
  `set` fixed, `order` arrangement, `case` legal, `live` alive) and 2 B2
  senses (`paper` article, `matter` substance). Definitions and examples use
  short language at the entry's own level.
- `en:court:noun` had a basketball example mixed into the legal sense; it moved
  to `en:court-sport:noun` and the legal entry now has legal examples only.
- Base entries fixed because they described a different part of speech:
  `en:cover:noun` (protective cover), `en:pass:noun` (exam result),
  `en:play:noun` (activity for fun), `en:light:adjective` (full of light) and
  `en:set:verb` (begin). New entries carry the other senses.
- Doubts for a reviewer: `rest` (remainder), `room` (space), `order`
  (arrangement) and `wear` (damage) are tagged uncountable; `ring` (phone) is
  British-leaning; `live` (not dead) is rarer than `live` (broadcast) and needs
  a frequency check; `fire` (shoot) is labelled B1 but some lists put it at B2.
- Existing entries with a definition from the wrong part of speech were left
  untouched when they were outside this pass: `en:mean:noun`,
  `en:look:adjective`, `en:show:adjective`, `en:land:noun` and
  `en:support:noun` (technical help only). `en:point:noun` is also narrow
  (`critical moment`), with the common senses split into `point-idea` and
  `point-score`. They should be fixed in a cleanup task, not silently changed
  here.
- `challenge` and `agency` already had a `sense` slug but no label; they were
  left untouched because they were outside this task.

### Gendered forms

- French `moral` → `moraux` (masculine plural) and `préventif` → `préventifs`
  are irregular in spelling but standard; `durable`, `civique`, `chronique`
  and `éthique` are invariable in gender.
- Italian `-ista` adjectives (`ottimista`, `pessimista`, `entusiasta`,
  `egoista`) have `-isti` masculine plural and `-iste` feminine plural.
  Invariable `-e` adjectives repeat the same form.
- Russian uses `plural_form` for the single plural (`устойчивые`), not the
  Romance `masculine_plural`/`feminine_plural` pair.
- Greek now uses all five fields, including the new `neuter_plural`
  (`πολιτικά`, `βιώσιμα`). The forms are nominative only.

## Excluded on purpose

- Multi-word headwords (`future plans`, `inner peace`, `small print`) and all
  idioms: the first pass is single-word lemmas only.
- Entries whose `form` does not match the headword in the existing data (for
  example `en:kindness:adjective`, `en:tact:adjective`, `it:prevention:noun`).
  Annotating those would have required fixing the part of speech and the id,
  which is out of scope for this task.
