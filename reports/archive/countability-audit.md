# Countability Audit Report

This report contains an audit of all noun entries (`form == "noun"`) across `vocabulary/en/` following the addition of the `countability` schema field and associated validation rules.

Total Noun Entries Audited: **3538**

---

## 1. Summary of Audit Findings

1. **Entries Missing `countability` Field**: All **3538** noun entries currently in `vocabulary/en/` lack the required `countability` field because it was newly introduced in the schema.
2. **Entries Requiring Non-Countable / Special Classification**: Numerous entries currently have a `plural_form` set despite representing uncountable nouns, pluralia tantum, invariable nouns, or false plurals.
3. **Countable Nouns with Suspicious or Naive Plurals**: Multiple entries have naive or incorrect plural forms generated by appending `+s`, `+es`, or `+ses` to words that are irregular or already pluralized.
