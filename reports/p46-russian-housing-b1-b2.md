# P46 Russian B1/B2 housing definitions and examples
P46 completed the Russian housing theme with definitions and examples for **61 entries: 45 B1 and 16 B2**.
## Scope and level
B1 definitions explain rental, building and maintenance terms in clear Russian. B1 examples use simple past, future or a short subordinate clause.
B2 definitions are more precise and abstract. B2 examples use passive forms, relative clauses or causal links.
## Sources checked
- Russian Wiktionary and standard explanatory dictionaries for spelling, gender and meaning
- Existing Russian B1/B2 entries for register and sentence length
- Russian grammar for noun gender, cases, agreement, verb aspect and subordinate clauses
## Verification
- Every definition is in Russian and describes the headword.
- Every example is in Russian, is B1/B2-appropriate, and contains the headword.
- No definition repeats the headword.
- No example uses a fixed template.
- One nonstandard headword was held back and recorded in `reports/needs-review/`.
