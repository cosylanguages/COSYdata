# P42 Greek A2 housing definitions and examples
P42 added definitions and examples for **100 Greek housing entries at A2**.
## Scope and level
The batch covers A2 housing vocabulary: rental terms, building types, rooms, furniture, appliances, maintenance and household actions. Definitions are short and concrete; examples are one sentence with present tense or simple past.
## Sources checked
- Greek Wiktionary and standard Modern Greek dictionaries for spelling, gender and meaning
- Existing Greek A2 entries for register and sentence length
- Modern Greek grammar for articles, gender, agreement and verb forms
## Verification
- Every definition is in Greek and describes the headword.
- Every example is in Greek, is A2-appropriate, and contains the headword.
- No definition repeats the headword.
- No example uses a fixed template.
- One entry was held back and recorded in `reports/needs-review/`.
