# P43 Greek B1/B2 housing definitions and examples
P43 completed the Greek housing theme with definitions and examples for **64 entries: 46 B1 and 18 B2**.
## Scope and level
B1 definitions explain rental, building and maintenance terms in clear Modern Greek. B1 examples use simple past, future or a subordinate clause where needed.
B2 definitions are more precise and abstract. B2 examples use passive forms, relative clauses or causal links.
## Sources checked
- Greek Wiktionary and standard Modern Greek dictionaries for spelling, gender and meaning
- Existing Greek B1/B2 entries for register and sentence length
- Modern Greek grammar for articles, gender, agreement, verb forms and subordinate clauses
## Verification
- Every definition is in Greek and describes the headword.
- Every example is in Greek, is B1/B2-appropriate, and contains the headword.
- No definition repeats the headword.
- No example uses a fixed template.
- One nonstandard headword was held back and recorded in `reports/needs-review/`.
